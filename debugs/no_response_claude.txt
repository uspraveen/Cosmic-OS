 I verified on the VM that both routes were still healthy after the Perplexity fix:

  - Haiku completed with route="haiku" and streamed thinking + response chunks.
  - Opus completed with route="opus" and streamed thinking + response chunks.
  - Perplexity also worked with the real provider call after the normalization fix.

  So the regression was back in the desktop renderer. The issue was a race in App.tsx: Haiku and Opus emit response.thinking.chunk early, and the UI was
  still waiting for Electron main to generate/return the request_id. That let early thinking events bind to the wrong assistant bubble before the new turn
  had a reserved slot. Perplexity was less affected because its stream shape is different.

  I fixed it by making the desktop reserve the assistant slot before the stream starts:

  - renderer now creates the request_id itself
  - it creates and binds the assistant placeholder immediately
  - Electron main / preload / connection manager now accept a caller-provided request_id
